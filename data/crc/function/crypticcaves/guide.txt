#spawnpoint 

- spawn at the *marker* with tag 'ccSpawnPos'
- spawn platform 3x3 with center as *marker* with tag 'ccSpawnPlatform'


#gravel placing spots

- marked using *markers* with tag 'ccGravelPlacer'


#gravel timer

- place *markers* at the base of a timer, and another block lower, with tag 'ccGravelTimer'


#respawn point

- players respawn at the *marker* of tag 'ccRespawnPoint'


#exit

- players exit at the *markers* with tag 'ccExit'


#rooms

- rooms are randomised with *markers* placed at the north-west bottom corner (21x21x21 pastes south-west) with tag 'ccRoomSpawn'


#building rooms

- rooms are marked with structure blocks, save with 'roomN'
