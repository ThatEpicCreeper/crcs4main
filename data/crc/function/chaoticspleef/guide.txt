
#spawnpoint (lobby)
- defined with *marker* of tag 'csSpawn'

#map spawning
- defined with a *marker* of tag 'csGameSpawn'
- this ideally should include the map that covers a 6-7 block radius minimum, it will spread players around the marker when the game starts

#map copying
- define the center of the map to copy placed exactly on the *marker* of 'csGameSpawn', plus 100 on the x-axis. (eg. if csGameSpawn is at 123 45 67, then place center of map at 223 45 67)
- will copy a 31x31x31 (31^3) volume to the actual arena (must fit map inside this)

#tnt & mob spawning
- define the center of the tnt spawn area with a *marker* of tag 'csChaosSpawn'
- ideally ~20 blocks above center of map from ground level
