
#spawnpoint
- defined with armor stand of tag 'hrStartPoint'

#checkpoints
- defined with armor stand of tag 'hrCheckpointN', where N is the checkpoint number

#finish line
- defined with armor stand of tag 'hrFinishLine'

#special blocks
- gold block = mini speed
- diamond block = big speed
- purpur block = mega speed
- emerald block = jump boost
- coal block = slowness
- chiseled quartz = levitation

#special modes
- trident giver is defined with armor stand 'giveTrident'
- trident remover is defined with armor stand 'removeTrident'

- elytra giver is defined with armor stand 'giveElytra'
- elytra is automatically removed if a player is too close to the ground.

- boats are given when a player does not have a boat in their inventory, and is far away from an existing boat.
- the region of a boat must be manually defined, as it is different in every map.
